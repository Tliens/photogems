FAQ

Can I download the photos?

Yes. Open any photo and click Download to save the full-resolution file. All photos are free for personal use.

Are the photos free to use?

Photos are free to download and use for personal, non-commercial purposes. For commercial use, please contact the author first.

Who made this gallery?

PhotoGems is the personal photo gallery of KuiGe (魁歌), curated by hand and served as a static site.

How often is the gallery updated?

New photos are added whenever the collection grows. The Updated date on the homepage reflects the latest sync.

What does the codename “Lantai” mean?

Lantai (兰台) was the imperial archive of the Han dynasty. This gallery keeps the same spirit: a quiet hall where images are collected and cared for.
